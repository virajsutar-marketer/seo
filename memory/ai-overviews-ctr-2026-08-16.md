# AI Overviews cut organic CTR by roughly a third, zero-click search hits 68%

**Date researched:** 2026-08-16
**Sources:**
- [AI Overviews Reduce Clicks by 34.5%](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overviews-reduce-clicks/), Ahrefs
- [Google zero-click searches reach 68% in early 2026: Study](https://searchengineland.com/google-zero-click-searches-2026-study-479717), Search Engine Land
- [Google AI Overviews CTR shows early signs of recovery: Study](https://searchengineland.com/google-ai-overviews-ctr-recovery-study-475566), Search Engine Land

## Finding

An Ahrefs study of 300,000 Google searches found that when an AI Overview appears at the top of results, average organic CTR drops by 34.5%. Separately, zero-click searches reached 68% of US Google queries in early 2026, up from about 60.45% two years earlier, meaning fewer than a third of Google searches now send a click to any website. There is a partial recovery signal too: after bottoming out at 1.3% in December 2025, CTR on AI Overviews themselves climbed to 2.4% by February 2026, an 85% jump in two months, though that still sits well below the roughly 3.3% CTR on searches with no AI Overview present. When an AI Overview cites a given page, that page earns roughly 2.1% CTR versus roughly 0.9% for an uncited page on the same results page.

## Why it matters for SEO/AEO/GEO work

This is the concrete, current-data backing for the playbook's repeated warning (Section 7.4, Section 16.2) that a page can rank position 1 with strong impressions yet earn near-zero clicks because an AI answer surface is satisfying the query directly. Treat a CTR drop coinciding with AI Overview appearance as a candidate positional artifact to rule in or out during any RCA (see the `ranking-rca` sub-skill) before "fixing" it with a title/meta rewrite. It also strengthens the case in the `aeo-geo-citations` sub-skill for tracking citation frequency as a KPI distinct from click volume: getting cited inside the AI Overview roughly doubles the residual CTR compared to not being cited, even though overall click volume on the query has collapsed either way.

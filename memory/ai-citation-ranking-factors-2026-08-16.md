# Brand mentions correlate with AI citations far more strongly than backlinks; most top-cited pages are off-limits to brand SEO

**Date researched:** 2026-08-16
**Source:** [What Actually Gets You Cited in AI Search (2026 Data)](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/ai-search-citation-ranking-factors-2026-data-study), Digital Applied, citing Ahrefs and Zyppy research

## Finding

Ahrefs' analysis of 75,000 brands found that brand web mentions correlate with AI citation rates at 0.664, roughly three times stronger than the 0.218 correlation for backlinks. Separately, Ahrefs found that 67% of the top 1,000 pages ChatGPT cites are sources a brand cannot directly influence through its own SEO work: Wikipedia, government and educational institutions, Apple's App Store, major news media, and other encyclopedic references. A May 2026 meta-analysis by Cyrus Shepard (Zyppy), synthesizing 54 experiments, patents, and case studies, scored 23 factors for generative engine optimization. Cross-platform citation overlap is low: only about 11% of domains were cited by both ChatGPT and Perplexity across 680 million citations analyzed by Averi, and Ahrefs found roughly 12% overlap between AI citations and Google's own top-10 results for the same query.

## Why it matters for SEO/AEO/GEO work

This reframes link building for AI-citation purposes: earned brand mentions (being named and discussed across the web, even without a link) now correlate with citation far more strongly than the classic backlink-authority signal the `SEO-PLAYBOOK.md` link-building section (17) is built around. It also sets an honest expectation for the `aeo-geo-citations` sub-skill's measurement loop: since most of what ChatGPT actually cites is non-brand-controlled reference content, and citation overlap between AI engines and traditional Google top-10 is low (about 12%), a site should not assume traditional SEO wins translate into AI citation wins, and the fixed prompt-bank measurement loop needs to be tracked as its own KPI set, not inferred from ranking position.

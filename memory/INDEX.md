# Memory Index

Dated research notes on real, current SEO/AEO/GEO industry findings. Each note cites a real source with a real URL retrieved via web search on the date in the filename. Use these to check whether a playbook rule or threshold needs updating in light of a confirmed, current industry change, not as a replacement for `SEO-PLAYBOOK.md` itself.

- [`algorithm-volatility-august-2026-2026-08-16.md`](./algorithm-volatility-august-2026-2026-08-16.md): Unconfirmed but widely reported August 2026 ranking volatility; pages with weak originality/thin evidence lost visibility, pages with original data/expertise held or gained. Source: Search Engine Watch.
- [`ai-overviews-ctr-2026-08-16.md`](./ai-overviews-ctr-2026-08-16.md): AI Overviews cut organic CTR by 34.5% on average; zero-click searches hit 68% of US Google queries in early 2026; being cited inside an AI Overview roughly doubles residual CTR versus not being cited. Sources: Ahrefs, Search Engine Land.
- [`ai-citation-ranking-factors-2026-08-16.md`](./ai-citation-ranking-factors-2026-08-16.md): Brand web mentions correlate with AI citation rate at 0.664 versus 0.218 for backlinks; 67% of ChatGPT's top-cited pages are non-brand-controlled reference sources; citation overlap between AI engines and Google's top 10 is only about 12%. Source: Digital Applied, citing Ahrefs and Zyppy.
- [`structured-data-faq-retirement-2026-08-16.md`](./structured-data-faq-retirement-2026-08-16.md): FAQ rich results stopped appearing in Google Search as of May 7, 2026; schema's role is shifting from a SERP-display trigger to an AI trust/entity-verification signal. Source: Search Engine Journal.
- [`local-seo-gbp-changes-2026-08-16.md`](./local-seo-gbp-changes-2026-08-16.md): GBP profile completeness is now an explicit ranking input; review recency now outweighs raw review volume; the GBP Q&A feature was discontinued and replaced by AI-driven "Ask Maps." Source: PinMeTo.
- [`core-web-vitals-inp-update-2026-08-16.md`](./core-web-vitals-inp-update-2026-08-16.md): INP measurement was tightened to capture sustained interaction latency across a page's full lifecycle, not just first input; only 55.9% of tracked origins pass all three Core Web Vitals as of the May 2026 CrUX release. Source: WebVitals.tools.
- [`spam-policy-june-2026-update-2026-08-16.md`](./spam-policy-june-2026-update-2026-08-16.md): Google's June 2026 spam update enforced scaled content abuse, cloaking, and keyword stuffing (explicitly excluding link spam and site-reputation abuse); AI-assisted content is judged on usefulness, not production method. Source: Semrush.

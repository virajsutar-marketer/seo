# Google's June 2026 spam update enforces scaled content abuse, cloaking, and keyword stuffing (excludes link spam/site-reputation abuse)

**Date researched:** 2026-08-16
**Source:** [Google completes its June 2026 spam update rollout](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-completes-spam-update-rollout/), Semrush

## Finding

Google released its June 2026 spam update on June 24, 2026, with the rollout completing globally, across all languages, on June 26. The update specifically targets violations of Google's spam policies excluding link spam and site-reputation abuse, with enforcement concentrated on content-level tactics: scaled content abuse, cloaking, and keyword stuffing. Scaled content abuse, producing large volumes of pages with little value to users, has been Google's most aggressively policed violation since it was introduced with the March 2024 core and spam updates, and increasingly overlaps with unedited, mass-produced AI content. Google's stated evaluation standard is the usefulness of the content, not the production method: AI-assisted content that is edited, accurate, and genuinely helpful remains within policy.

## Why it matters for SEO/AEO/GEO work

This is current confirmation that the spam-policy guardrails already documented in `SEO-PLAYBOOK.md` Section 8.4 and the pSEO gating criteria in Section 4.4 are actively enforced, not theoretical: an update specifically naming scaled content abuse rolled out and completed in June 2026. Any templated or programmatic content build (an error-message library, a location-page series, a comparison-page batch) should be re-gated against "is there real, specific, sourced substance behind this one page" before scaling, since that exact failure mode is what this update targets. The explicit exclusion of link spam and site-reputation abuse from this particular update also means those two categories should still be checked separately, they were not the focus of the June 2026 enforcement pass.
